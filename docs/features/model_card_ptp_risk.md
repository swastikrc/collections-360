# Model card: promise risk (ptp-risk-1.0)

Generated by `python -m c360.models.ptp_risk train` on 2026-10-04T16:06:29. Nothing here is typed by hand.

## What it predicts

- Target: 1 if the promise was broken or only partly kept, 0 if kept in full; open and cancelled promises have no label.
- Unit: one promise to pay, scored as of the end of the call in which it was made.
- Use: advice inside Layer 3's next best action. A person approves, edits or rejects every action; the score never acts on its own, and a high score leads to a supportive step (reminder by call, affordability check, smaller plan), never to more pressure.

## Limitations

- Logistic regression: effects are additive; the boosted comparison above shows how much a non-linear model would add.
- The break rate shifted sharply from May 2026; probabilities from a model trained before a shift run low. Retrain monthly and watch mean predicted vs actual.
- Text patterns were tuned on the 500 public labelled notes (outputs/features/text_feature_check.md); no separate held-out text set exists.
- Fairness: see `docs/features/fairness_report.md` (measurement only; protected attributes never enter the model).
