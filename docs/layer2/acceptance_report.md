## 8. Known limitations and next steps

- Five metrics have no official definition and stay labelled placeholders (`promise_count`, `payment_on_contact_amount`, `hardship_offer_acceptance_rate`, `open_case_count`, `past_due_amount`).
- `account_monthly_snapshot` holds quarter-end snapshots only: a roll rate inside one quarter has no start/end pair.
- No document covers legal action; such questions can only be refused or answered as 'not covered'.
- Open decisions (CHANGELOG): 'last N days' inclusive; customer names reaching the model via get_customer and transcript text; Layer 3's use of vulnerability flags.
- Out of scope (SPEC §12): dual-LLM summariser for search_text, build-id consistency check between DuckDB and MongoDB, verifier pass, answer cache, minimum group size suppression. Multi-agent mode: SPEC_LAYER2_MULTIAGENT.md (not started).
